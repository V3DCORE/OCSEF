THE PROBLEM ON YOUR DESK

Every gadget hides metals dug from the ground. Click each item to see what is inside, and who pays for it.

THE SOLUTION: URBAN MINING

Old electronics are full of gold, copper, cobalt and more. Recycling them lets us "urban mine" those metals and reuse them. That means less new mining, healthier ecosystems and less pressure for exploitative labor. Hover or tap a drop-off station.

The world made about 62 million tonnes of e-waste in 2022. Only about 22% was documented as recycled.

Stations are examples. Check your city's program for real drop-off sites. Stat: UN Global E-waste Monitor 2024.
